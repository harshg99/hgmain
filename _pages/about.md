I am a Ph.D. student at The University of Texas at Austin. My research develops video agents that search for relevant information, reason about events and human intent, and coordinate with people and other agents. Recent work includes long-video question answering, neuro-symbolic video reasoning, and multi-agent reinforcement learning.

I was previously advised by Dr. Guillaume Sartoretti and Dr. Marcelo H. Ang Jr. at the National University of Singapore, and Dr. Vijay Kumar at the University of Pennsylvania. Outside the lab, I enjoy tennis, AI podcasts, and playing the keyboard.
